# Third-party visual assets

The selected SVG assets are stored locally in `src/assets/icons/`.

- Technology logos, GitHub and LinkedIn: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon),
  revision `7330accdbc47e2dc0c19789a48533c4a3c50fe58`, from `icons/`.
  MIT license: `src/assets/icons/DEVICON-LICENSE.txt`.
- Colombian, Australian, United States and Chilean flags: [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons),
  revision `086f7e97d657358203916dbe84f61c2bccaa81eb`, from `flags/4x3/`.
  MIT license: `src/assets/icons/FLAG-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- OpenAI (used for ChatGPT), GitHub Copilot and WhatsApp: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.21.0),
  version `13.21.0`, from `icons/`. CC0 1.0 license:
  `src/assets/icons/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- Casanare Devs: the [official logo](https://casanare.dev/logo.svg), supplied by the
  portfolio owner as the community's logo source. Stored without graphic changes
  in `src/assets/icons/casanare-dev.svg` and used to identify the linked community.
  The community logo is not covered by the project's MIT license.

- Company logos in the Journey timeline: the marks of the employers listed there,
  stored in `src/assets/companies/` and mapped in `CompanyLogo.jsx`. The portfolio
  owner supplied where to find each one: the companies' own websites
  (agencia.deltasoluciones.tech, r8write.com, creandosoft.com, sistemasinteligentes.co),
  a HubSpot file host for LEGOPSTECH, and an inline SVG for Fix-U. They are copied as
  supplied, except that the Sistemas Inteligentes PNG is downscaled to 560 px wide to
  keep the page light. NTICS has no logo yet and shows a neutral building icon. These
  marks belong to their owners, are not covered by the project's MIT license, and only
  identify where the work was done.

The WhatsApp SVG uses the brand's green fill; its geometry is unchanged.

AWS service cards use the AWS family logo; Blade uses the Laravel framework logo.
OpenVidu/WebRTC and non-branded engineering practices use local neutral symbols.

Brand logos identify the relevant technologies or profiles. They do not imply
endorsement. The original profile photographs were supplied by the portfolio owner.
